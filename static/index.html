      A book in one sentence

      Give it a subject.
      Watch a book assemble itself.

      Bookwright stages the work the way an author does: one model designs the argument, another writes each chapter against a running bible of what came before, and a third keeps the book from contradicting itself. Then it typesets.

      • 01 Architect — parts, chapters, beats
      • 02 Writer — chapters, streamed as they land
      • 03 Editor — summaries + glossary for continuity
      • 04 Composer — Markdown, HTML, EPUB, PDF
